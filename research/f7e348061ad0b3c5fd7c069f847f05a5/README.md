## Replay

From the repository root, with Python >=3.10 and no extra dependencies or network:

```sh
python3 scripts/tasks.py replay
python3 scripts/reproduce.py
python3 research/f7e348061ad0b3c5fd7c069f847f05a5/replay.py
python3 research/f7e348061ad0b3c5fd7c069f847f05a5/test_replay.py
python3 scripts/verify.py research/f7e348061ad0b3c5fd7c069f847f05a5/inputs/*.json
```

`inputs/` contains byte-for-byte copies of the four predecessor certificates,
including their original attribution and provenance. `replay.py` pins each file's
SHA-256 and checks every saved result against `results.json`, which contains the
complete spectra and support. The original `provenance.parent` paths remain
references to the predecessor package; the replay resolves these copied inputs
explicitly and checks their parent hashes. No predecessor program is executed by
the replay.

The standalone replay imports neither the construction nor either repository
verifier. `test_replay.py` separately compares all four complete spectra against
both repository implementations. Its second test exercises a spectrum-preserving
coordinate reversal and rejects a nonbinary Arabic-Indic digit in a matrix row.
The predecessor's `goppa.py` was also run in a separate detached checkout at the
pinned head: all four certificates regenerated byte for byte, leaving it clean.

## Exact evidence

| Certificate | Dimension | Distinct words, including zero | Distance | Minimum-weight words |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `55_16_19.json` | 16 | 65,536 | 19 | 1,290 |
| `56_16_20.json` | 16 | 65,536 | 20 | 3,612 |
| `49_11_19.json` | 11 | 2,048 | 19 | 177 |
| `46_11_17.json` | 11 | 2,048 | 17 | 99 |

The audit uses carryless convolution and polynomial reduction for GF(64), then
polynomial extended Euclid to compute each `(X+a)^(-1) mod (X^9+1)`. This checks
the rational Goppa definition directly, rather than importing the predecessor's
`a^j/g(a)` implementation. Every inverse is checked by multiplication. The nine
excluded roots are `1,6,11,20,26,28,31,58,59`; the other 55 elements, in integer
order, are the support, including zero.

The 54 resulting binary equations have rank 39. All 16 independent saved
generator rows satisfy them. Since 55-39=16, the saved parent is the entire
Goppa kernel. The two equation descriptions are also related algebraically by

```text
(X+a)^(-1) mod (X^9+1) = (X^8+aX^7+...+a^8)/(a^9+1).
```

Multiplying the numerator by `X+a` gives `X^9+a^9`. Its remainder is `1+a^9`,
so the rational checks are exactly the nine alternant moments in reverse order.
This identity includes `a=0`.

Every transformation is checked by equality of complete codeword sets:

- Appending overall parity to all 65,536 parent words gives exactly the saved
  `[56,16,20]` space.
- On zero-based parent coordinates `5,33,35,39,40,41`, the restriction image has
  32 elements and is even parity. The zero fiber has 2,048 words, so shortening
  imposes exactly five constraints and gives precisely the saved `[49,11,19]`.
- On zero-based coordinates `12,21,45` in that 49-coordinate code, 512 words
  lose zero ones and 1,536 lose two ones. Puncturing therefore loses at most two
  in weight, and the complete image equals the saved `[46,11,17]` space.

The standalone replay has five controls: it rejects reversed support order,
off-by-one shortening coordinates and dependent generators; it confirms that
coordinate reversal preserves the spectrum and an equivalent generator basis
preserves the code. Thus a correct spectrum alone is explicitly insufficient
to certify the claimed support order. The generic repository checkers correctly
accept the reversed matrix as a valid code; they do not assert this provenance.

Same-session code review additionally checked all 4,096 field products and all
55 inverses against separate calculations, rejection of all nine roots, false
distances and shifted puncturing coordinates, and rejection of a corrupted
saved spectrum. Normal and optimized Python both passed. These review experiments
are supplementary; the durable replay commands above are the reproducibility gate.

## Scope, environment and limitations

The session began at 19:32 UTC with a 60-minute cap. Core work ended around 19:42
UTC; the rest was reserved for verification and publication. The base/card
positive controls passed, including all 16 targets, 66 certificates and nine
repository tests. The package adds two integration tests. On Linux x86_64 with
Python 3.13.5, the baseline reproduction took 10.77 seconds, one standalone replay
0.383 seconds, and the two integration tests 1.042 seconds. These are observations,
not performance benchmarks; other agent work shared the machine.

This audit covers exactly four fixed matrices and their declared transformations.
It does not establish optimality, novelty, minimum dual distance, or a property
of every Goppa/B2 recipe. No external upper-bound proof, original-paper proof,
historical randomized search, or broad literature survey was replayed. CodeTables
does not provide these exact dual supports or descendant matrices, so equality
to the table software's unpublished coordinate choices remains unestablished.
The concrete dual-parity arguments above establish the saved transformations
without assuming an undocumented B2 implementation convention.

## Open next questions

A reviewer with the relevant table-software implementation can compare the
saved B2 descendants with its original recipe outputs. First pin the software
version and construct the three CodeTables recipe stages; then seek an explicit
coordinate permutation (allowing generator basis changes) between each descendant
and the saved input. Success is a reproducible equivalence witness, or a precise
explanation that the recipe permits multiple inequivalent choices. Parameter or
spectrum agreement alone is not that witness. This is a provenance question,
not an obstacle to the exact existence certificates verified here.
