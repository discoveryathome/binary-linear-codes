# Literature audit, 2026-10-20

The question is the unrestricted binary **linear** optimum d_max(n,k). A best-known construction is a lower bound; a classification, analytic bound, or exhaustive nonexistence proof may supply an upper bound. Optimal within LCD, self-dual, cyclic, minimal, quantum, or locally repairable families is not unrestricted optimality.

## Scope and unresolved checks

The complete table, recent explicit parent parameters, and the cited upper-bound corrections were processed **before final target selection**. There is no exhaustive census of all papers. Targeted searches for the proposed improvement triples of the 16 final cases found no additional applicable constructions or nonexistence results; absence of a search hit is not a proof of novelty or openness. Recheck current literature and table-maintainer records before any record claim.

The six superseded initial targets were (35,10), (36,10), (36,11), (37,11), (37,12), and (38,12). Their constructions remain useful controls. The preliminary run is archived under `results/preaudit/` and excluded from final discovery-rate denominators. The final experiment runs again on the corrected selection.

The lower-bound frontier is not the set of matrices our importer understands. Unsupported Construction X, B2, Goppa, and Zinoviev recipes remain novelty exclusions even when no local witness is available. `results/reconstruction-audit.json` records deferred recipes. This prevents importer limitations from manufacturing records.
